How it works

One conversation,
carried through your days.

You sign up here and talk to Zeph in Telegram. This site is where you see what it remembers and decide how it behaves. Five steps, most of them one tap.

From sign-up to check-in

What happens,
in order.

Steps one and two happen once. The rest is the conversation itself.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    About two minutes

    Sign up on this site and answer a few short questions: what to call you, what brings you here, and whether Zeph may remember things and check in on you.

  2. 2

    Connect Telegram

    One tap

    One tap links your account to Zeph on Telegram. Every conversation happens there, so there is no new app to learn.

  3. 3

    Talk it through

    Whenever you like

    Message Zeph the way you would message a thoughtful friend. It asks questions, listens, and disagrees when that is more useful than agreeing. It draws on philosophy, psychology and mindfulness, in plain words.

  4. 4

    It remembers, with your permission

    Your choice

    If you allow it, Zeph keeps the details that matter, like your goals and the people you mention, so you never start from zero. You can read every memory and delete any of them.

  5. 5

    It checks in, if you want it to

    Your choice

    Turn check-ins on and Zeph will sometimes message you first. You set how often at most and your quiet hours. Zeph chooses the moment, and sometimes stays quiet to give you space.

Your dashboard
