Remember meaningful details

So you don't have to repeat yourself, I can keep meaningful details: your goals, what helps you, the people you mention.

You can see what I remember on your Dashboard and delete any of it, or tell me "forget that" in chat.

I also keep a few private notes to follow up with you, like something to ask about later. You won't see these, and Delete My Data removes them along with everything else.

Look at my photos

When off, Zeph won't look at or remember your photos. Every photo is still checked for safety, so Zeph can share help if one looks worrying. Nothing from that check is kept.

When on, Zeph looks at the photos you send and may keep the ones that matter to you, listed on your Memories page.

How direct Zeph is

How soon and how plainly Zeph tells you what it thinks is really going on, and how it asks you things. Gentle asks less often and leaves more time between questions; direct asks sooner and more to the point. Whatever you pick, Zeph gives its view when you ask, backs off when you’d rather not answer, and takes extra care when things are hard.

Keep noticing after I disagree

When Zeph tells you something it has noticed about you and you disagree, it never brings it up again unless you do.

When on, Zeph still quietly notes what you say that fits it, in case it holds up after all. When off, it notes nothing new for it. Every quote Zeph keeps is listed on your Memories page, where you can delete any of them.

Delete my data

Removes your messages, memories, patterns, the private notes Zeph keeps to follow up with you, Day Summaries, your stickers, Check-in history and Telegram Connection. Your account stays, so you can start fresh.

Messages are otherwise kept for 30 days. Quotes Zeph keeps as evidence stay until you delete them.

Delete account

Erases everything above plus your account. Only possible here on the Dashboard. This can't be undone.
