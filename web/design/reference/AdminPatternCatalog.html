15 Pattern Types

What it is

  • IDCONF.AVOID.BELONG
  • DefinitionWhen disagreement arises with someone whose approval matters, the person suppresses, delays or dilutes their own position. The driver is fear that conflict will damage the relationship, not lack of opinion.
  • IfDisagreement with a valued other
  • ThenWithholds or softens own view
  • FunctionTo protect belonging
  • Based onYoung's subjugation and approval-seeking schema themes; Anxious attachment dimension (ECR-R); HEXACO agreeableness facets (gentleness, flexibility); Schwartz conformity and benevolence values

Look-alikes

What it can be mistaken for, and what tells them apart.

  • Performance-contingent self-worth
    Keeps quiet to protect the relationship; the other fears criticism as a verdict on their competence
    ACH.WORTH.PERF
  • Control-seeking under uncertainty
    Keeps quiet because closeness is at stake; the other is uneasy about any unknown outcome, closeness or not
    CTRL.UNCERT.PLAN
  • Saying yes and over-giving to feel needed
    Says yes to avoid the friction of saying no; the other says yes to be needed and keep a place
    APPR.GIVE.NEEDED
  • Taking on other people's moods as theirs to fix
    Stays quiet about their own view; the other acts to fix the other's mood
    REL.MOOD.FIX
  • What anyone would do
    Keeping quiet over small things with strangers or people in charge
    Carries no weight

Wording

What Zeph words a shared Pattern and its Prediction from.

  • PatternYou seem to protect closeness by keeping disagreements small, even when they matter to you.
  • Prediction 1Next time {person} asks for something you'd rather not do, notice whether you say yes before you've decided.
  • Prediction 2Next time you go along with {person} on something, notice how you feel about it a few hours later.
  • Prediction 3Next time you disagree with someone you're not close to, like {person}, notice whether saying so feels any easier.
